Long traps could use progressively longer opening drills: recognize the setup,
learn the critical response, then rehearse the important continuation. Each
chapter still replays from the initial position. Avoid memorizing obvious
clean-up; distinguish an instructive forced sequence from an arbitrary finish.
This may fit a separate advanced Traps course or a future Mastery course.
