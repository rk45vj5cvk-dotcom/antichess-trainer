# Wizard Opening training module

Train White. Every chapter replays from the standard initial Antichess position.
Quiz chapters are excluded. Only supplied main lines become drills; side variations remain in wizard-source.pgn.
Early Black deviations described in the source are connected by legally validated opening prefixes.
Long lines grow through checkpoints, then their complete supplied continuation. A checkpoint is not necessarily a win.

The former chapter 5 (source 3, full continuation) is omitted as obvious clean-up; its earlier opening checkpoints remain.
